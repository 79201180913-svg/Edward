# v0.8.1 Integration Contract

The v0.8.1 multifactor layer is an analytical overlay over v0.8.

## Stable outputs
- `AnalysisResult` remains the v0.8-compatible public analysis result.
- `OpportunityResult` remains the v0.8-compatible downstream result.
- Decision and execution contracts remain unchanged.

## New internal evidence
The multifactor layer can consume optional contract-aligned data for:
- fundamentals;
- order book and trades;
- buy/sell candle volume;
- T-Invest signals;
- corporate events/reports;
- dividends;
- insider activity;
- trading sessions;
- instrument risk rates;
- portfolio/operations;
- derivatives.

## Decision influence
Evidence affects opportunity score and confidence through an explicit overlay. Hard blocks are limited to execution-critical conditions such as clearing, extreme instrument risk and critical near-term event risk.

## Missing-data policy
Optional unavailable sources remain unavailable and contribute zero evidence rather than an artificial neutral signal.

## External analyst forecast policy
Analyst consensus and opaque external target-price/probability estimates are deliberately excluded from v0.8.1 automatic evidence.
