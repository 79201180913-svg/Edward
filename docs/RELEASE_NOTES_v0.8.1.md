# Edward v0.8.1

Multi-Factor Evidence & Market Intelligence.

This release extends v0.8 analysis with contract-available market evidence while preserving external v0.8/v0.7 contracts.

Excluded from automatic decisions: analyst consensus, analyst target prices, and opaque external analyst probabilities.

Implemented factor families include fundamentals, market microstructure, buy/sell volume pressure, T-Invest signals, corporate events, dividends, insider activity, trading sessions, instrument risk rates, portfolio operations, and derivatives enrichment.
